The CISCM approach

Six principles behind every decision

End-to-end scope

Strategy, planning, sourcing, operations, logistics, finance, data and trust treated as one connected system.

A Trust current

Compliance, risk, ethics and sustainability are core professional competence, not an add-on.

Performance-based standards

Every competency unit is written as observable performance criteria.

Proficiency over familiarity

Recognition depends on the level of capability shown, not on topics covered.

Open preparation routes

No single mandatory course: experience, self-study, employers, academia or providers.

Evidence-based recognition

Every status is backed by verifiable evidence and limited to its approved scope.
