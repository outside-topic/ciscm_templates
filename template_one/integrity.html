Public claims & brand use

Say exactly what has been approved — no more and no less

Holders of CISCM recognition and accredited entities must describe that recognition accurately in all public communications, marketing and official documents.

1

Exact wording only

Use only the precise title, grade or status formally awarded.

2

No implied endorsement

Never suggest approval or recognition that was not granted.

3

Brand and mark restriction

No CISCM branding, logos or marks without written permission.

4

No overstated claims

Competence, scope or level claims never exceed the formal recognition.

5

Immediate correction

Remove or update claims at once on suspension, expiry or withdrawal.

6

Verifiability

Every public claim must be checkable through CISCM verification.

Permitted

  • • Stating the exact title, grade or status formally awarded
  • • Claims verifiable through CISCM verification
  • • Keeping claims current and matching the recognition
  • • Updating or removing claims when recognition lapses

Prohibited

  • • Implying endorsement, approval or recognition not granted
  • • Using CISCM logos, branding or marks without written permission
  • • Claiming competence, scope or level beyond the formal award
  • • Continuing claims after suspension, expiry or withdrawal
Complaints

Raise a concern about service, quality, conduct or public claims

Identity

Your name and contact details

Details

A clear description of the concern

Evidence

Supporting documents, where available

Subject

The person, credential or organisation

Possible outcomes

  • • Correction of public claims or marketing
  • • Suspension or withdrawal of recognition
  • • Additional evidence or training required
  • • Formal warning or reprimand
  • • Referral to another body if outside scope
  • • No action if not upheld
