Individual membership grades

Progression through competence, ethics and contribution

Membership is separate from certification.

Grade 1

Associate Member

Eligibility

Relevant learning and at least one year of relevant experience, or accepted competence evidence

Indicative evidence

CV, learning evidence, experience verification

Grade 2

Professional Member

Eligibility

Relevant competence and at least three years of progressive experience

Indicative evidence

Verified experience, competence statement, CPD and references

Grade 3

Chartered Member

Eligibility

Advanced competence, significant accountability, ethical standing and peer-reviewed evidence

Indicative evidence

Portfolio, references, professional review, leadership impact

Grade 4

Fellow

Eligibility

Sustained contribution, impact, leadership and professional service, normally over eight to ten years

Indicative evidence

Nomination or application, impact portfolio, references, panel review

Grade names, exact eligibility, approved post-nominals and any Chartered title are formally approved and legally reviewed before applications are accepted or designations authorised.

Organisational membership

Membership for organisations

Corporate Member

For employers developing supply chain capability: resources, events, competency support and preferred access to published services.

Academic or Training Partner Member

For education and training institutions seeking engagement and collaboration. This does not imply accreditation.

Public or Nonprofit Partner Member

For public, development, association and nonprofit bodies taking part in sector dialogue, learning and research.

Organisational membership does not, by itself, accredit an institution or programme, authorise delivery, approve trainers or permit certificate issuance. Each requires a separate documented decision.

Member obligations
