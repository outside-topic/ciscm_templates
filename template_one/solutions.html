Solutions for Organizations

For employers, education providers and corporate academies building real supply chain capability.

EmployersEducation ProvidersCorporate Academies
Employers

Turn the Body of Knowledge into a workforce capability system

Define role requirements, assess current capability, plan development and check whether learning improves performance.

Role and competency architecture

Roles defined by the competency units and proficiency levels they need — not topic lists, years of experience or software skills.

Capability assessment

Identify gaps across teams, functions and populations instead of relying on subjective ratings or completion data.

Development pathways

Link specific gaps to proficiency targets, suitable methods and evidence of application.

Succession and key-person risk

Find where critical capability sits with individuals and build progression routes to reduce dependency.

Recruitment and interview design

Selection built on competency evidence and scenario assessment rather than generic questions.

Corporate academy alignment

Connect internal learning to an external standard so outcomes are verifiable and portable.

Education providers

Build programmes that teach, assess and evidence real competence

Map programmes to the CISCM Body of Knowledge and seek recognition for coverage and quality. Recognition confirms that an approved programme covers stated units at a stated proficiency level — it never makes attendance compulsory for CISCM assessment.

What strong provider mapping shows

1The occupational problem and target audience
2Competencies and required proficiency
3Measurable learning outcomes
4Curriculum sequence, learning activities and time
5Assessment evidence for every outcome
6Faculty and assessor competence
7Delivery-mode equivalence and accessibility
8Quality review, performance evidence and change control

Provider support routes

  • Institutional or programme accreditation
  • Approved provider or delivery centre review
  • Curriculum review and benchmarking
  • Trainer and assessor recognition
  • Assessment blueprinting, rubric design and moderation
  • Certification administration and verification support
  • Quality-system and application-readiness guidance

Independence rule

Developmental review or consultancy is not accreditation. Where CISCM advises and later makes a recognition decision, independent reviewers and conflict safeguards are used. The provider remains responsible for its own system.
