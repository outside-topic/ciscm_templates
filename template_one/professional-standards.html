The four flows through every supply chain

Proficiency levels

Five cumulative levels

Each level assumes mastery of the levels below it. The step from Analytical to Architectural is the scarce step — and Level 4 is the governing standard for the Chartered tier.

See how credentials use the levels

Six cross-cutting threads

Assessed inside every domain

Ethical Judgement

Spotting ethical issues inside technical decisions and acting when commercial pressure conflicts with professional duty.

Data and Digital Fluency

Questioning what data measures, whether it is reliable and whether the analysis supports the conclusion.

Risk Awareness

Asking what happens if a design, dependency or assumption fails — at the moment of decision.

Sustainability Reasoning

Building environmental and social impact into everyday sourcing, logistics, planning and supplier decisions.

Global and Cultural Competence

Working across legal systems, cultures, languages and commercial norms without assuming one market fits all.

Commercial and Financial Reasoning

Framing operational proposals in cost, cash-flow, risk and capital-allocation terms.
