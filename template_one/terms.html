User Agreement & General Conditions

Agreement to Terms

These Terms govern your use of the CISCM website and its professional services. By accessing this website or submitting an application, you explicitly agree to use CISCM services honestly, responsibly, and in accordance with all applicable requirements.

Scope of Services

CISCM Services

CISCM provides professional credentials, membership, accreditation, learning, and related professional services.

Standards Note

Certification demonstrates assessed competence, while membership represents continuing professional standing. Accreditation is evidence-based and applies strictly to the approved scope.

User Obligations

Your Responsibilities

As a user or applicant, you must:

  • Provide accurate and authentic information
  • Comply with assessment & membership rules
  • Use CISCM credentials accurately
  • Respect CISCM intellectual property
  • Avoid misleading recognition claims

Note: CISCM may request additional evidence or take appropriate action where requirements are not met.
