CISCM

Service portfolio

Eleven services

Trainer and Facilitator Recognition

Subject competence, occupational experience, facilitation skill, engagement, ethics, CPD and observed performance.

Assessor and Moderator Recognition

Competence, independence, security, consistent judgement, sampling and ongoing development.

CPD Event Recognition

Relevance, outcomes, agenda, facilitator competence, duration, evidence and accurate CPD-hour claims.

Curriculum Review and Benchmarking

Relevance, coherence, industry alignment, coverage and assessment — with a gap report and priorities.

Assessment Design and Moderation

Blueprints, item review, case design, rubrics, standard setting and moderation, with confidentiality controls.

Certification Administration

Controlled certificates, digital credentials, replacements, register management and verification.

Corporate Training Solutions

Custom learning from needs analysis, role requirements and measurable outcomes.

Executive and Leadership Development

System design, decision-making, risk, resilience, sustainability, finance and transformation.

Competency Mapping

Role profiles, proficiency indicators, capability matrices, gap analyses and roadmaps.

Career and Job Readiness

Pathways, self-assessment, interview preparation and mentoring. No job, salary or immigration outcome is guaranteed.

Resources and Knowledge Hub

Reviewed guides, cases, tools, templates, recorded sessions, articles and communities.

Service engagement process

Seven steps, fully controlled

1

Discover

Purpose, users, constraints, risks, outcomes, decision authority

2

Scope

Deliverables, exclusions, responsibilities, milestones, fees, acceptance

3

Design

Method, expertise, data, quality checks, change control

4

Deliver

Approved personnel and controlled materials

5

Verify

Quality, acceptance criteria, feedback, open issues

6

Close

Final outputs, approved materials, retention

7

Follow up

Evaluate outcomes; corrective, renewal or next-stage support
