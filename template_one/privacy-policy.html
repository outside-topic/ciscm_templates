Privacy Policy

Chartered Institute of Supply Chain Management LLC

Chartered Institute of Supply Chain Management LLC

Our Commitment to Privacy

The Chartered Institute of Supply Chain Management LLC (CISCM) respects your privacy and is dedicated to protecting the personal and professional information entrusted to us. We collect only the information reasonably necessary for stated operational, educational, and accreditation purposes.

Collection Scope

Information We Collect

Depending on the service you use, CISCM may collect:

Personal & Contact Information

Contact details, identification records, and correspondence.

Education & Professional Experience

Academic background, work history, resumes, and qualifications.

Certification & Assessment Records

Examination results, competency evaluations, and submitted evidence.

Membership & CPD Records

Standing, grade, continuing professional development logs, and verifications.

Accreditation & Organizational Details

Provider documentation, facility information, and institutional data.

Applications, Complaints & Appeals Data

Formal applications, inquiries, and dispute records.

Purpose of Processing

How We Use Your Information

Information collected may be utilized to:

  • Process applications & assessments
  • Administer certification & membership
  • Conduct accreditation activities
  • Maintain credential & verification records
  • Manage complaints, appeals & inquiries
  • Maintain compliance, security & quality
Data Security

Confidentiality & Security

CISCM employs strict operational controls to protect identity, assessment, credential, financial, learner, and partner information. Access is strictly controlled based on role and purpose.

Strict Safeguard

Confidential data is never used for unrelated marketing, consultancy, research, or AI/model training without lawful authority and verified permission.

Data Rights

Your Information & Access

Where applicable, you have the right to request access to or correction of your personal data.

Record Retention

CISCM may retain records as necessary for ongoing credential verification, membership standing, accreditation auditing, legal obligations, and quality assurance.

Data Privacy Officer

Have Privacy Enquiries?

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, your data rights, or how your personal information is managed, please contact our data governance team.
