Who we are

Standards, credentials, learning and recognition — on one competence framework

CISCM is an independent professional organisation registered in the USA, specialising in supply chain, logistics, procurement and operations management. It develops standards, credentials, learning and recognition services.

We serve professionals, employers, training providers, corporate academies and partner institutions, combining a competence standard with learning, assessment, membership, accreditation, continuing development and organisational services.

The Body of Knowledge defines the domains, competency units, performance criteria and proficiency levels that every CISCM service uses for assessment, development and quality assurance.

CISCM

Our vision

To be a trusted professional learning and standards partner that supports capable, responsible, efficient and future-ready supply chain professionals.

Our mission

To define and assess supply chain competence, develop professionals and organisations through practical learning, recognise credible learning systems, and promote ethical, evidence-based, continuously improving practice.

Operating values

Eight values that govern how we work

Practical relevance

Standards and learning address real decisions and workplace consequences.

Integrity and accurate claims

Recognition is described exactly, backed by evidence and limited to approved scope.

Competence and evidence

Attendance, policy or reputation alone do not prove performance.

Learner and stakeholder protection

Entry, assessment, certification, complaints, data and partners are fair and transparent.

Accessibility and fairness

Multiple valid routes; reasonable adjustments remove barriers without changing the standard.

Industry currency

The framework responds to technology, regulation, trade, risk and sustainability change.

Impartiality

Commercial interest never determines accreditation, assessment, membership or complaint outcomes.

Continuous improvement

Data, feedback, complaints and monitoring lead to documented improvement.

Learning philosophy

The method may vary. The required outcome does not.

01

Any mode

Classroom, live virtual, online, blended and workplace learning are all supported.

02

Linked to decisions

Learning connects concepts to decisions, tools, cases, calculations, simulations and evidence of application.

03

No single route

Experience, self-study, employer development, academic study, approved providers — or a combination.
